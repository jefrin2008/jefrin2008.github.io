04 Projects

BOUNCE_BALL_DELUXE_v1.0.0

PythonPygameTkinter

A feature-packed brick-breaker for desktop, built with a Tkinter + Pygame hybrid. Smash through 3 levels, survive a boss fight on Level 3, collect power-ups, chain combos and spend your coins in the shop. Progress, coins and achievements are saved automatically between sessions.

  • 3 levels + boss: Level 3 is a boss arena with an HP bar and bomb attacks.
  • 7 power-ups: Wide, Multi-ball, Slow, Fire ball, Extra life, Laser paddle and Magnet.
  • Special bricks: golden, bomb and ice bricks, plus moving bricks.
  • Combo system: combos up to x30 and a Combo Fever score multiplier.
  • 4 difficulties: Easy, Normal, Hard and Insane change ball, brick and boss behaviour.
  • Shop: unlock 5 ball skins and 3 paddle skins with coins you earn.
  • Missions and achievements: a mission each level and 9 achievements to unlock.
  • Challenge mode: a timed, one-life run for score chasers.
  • Polish: particles, screen shake, 4 colour themes, sound with volume/mute and auto graphics quality.
  • Safe saves: JSON save file with high score; runs silently if no audio device is found.
